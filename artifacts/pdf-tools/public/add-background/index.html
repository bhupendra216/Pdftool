Add Background

Remove the existing background and add a new background to your image.

How it works

  1. Upload an image with the background you want to replace.
  2. Choose a transparent, solid-color, or custom background.
  3. Download the finished image as a PNG or JPG.

Why use this tool

Frequently asked questions

  • Does this run in my browser?

    Yes. The foreground is extracted locally in your browser, then composited onto the new background you choose.

  • What output formats are available?

    PNG is always available. JPG is available whenever the chosen background is not transparent.
