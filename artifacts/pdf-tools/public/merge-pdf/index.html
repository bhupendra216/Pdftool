Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDFs into a single organized file.

How it works

  1. Upload the PDF files you want to combine.
  2. Drag to reorder the files into the order you want them merged.
  3. Click Merge and download your combined PDF.

Frequently asked questions

  • How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    You can merge as many files as you need in a single batch. For very large batches, splitting into a couple of merges keeps things fast.

  • Will the page order be preserved?

    Yes, pages are merged in exactly the order you arrange the files, and each file&#39;s internal page order is preserved.
