How it works

  1. Upload the PDF you want to shrink.
  2. Pick a compression level: balanced, or maximum compression.
  3. Click Compress and download your smaller PDF.

Frequently asked questions

  • How much smaller will my file get?

    It depends on the content: PDFs with lots of high-resolution images shrink the most, while text-only PDFs are already small.
