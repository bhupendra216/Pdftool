Resume & CV Builder

Free online resume builder and CV maker that runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no upload.

About Resume & CV Builder

Create and edit a resume with sections for your summary, education, experience, skills, and more. Your resume is saved in this browser and is not uploaded; use the browser print dialog to print or save it as a PDF.

How to build a resume online

  1. Edit your details

    Replace the fictional sample contact information and update the summary, education, skills, and other sections.

  2. Organize the resume

    Choose Job or Higher study mode, reorder sections, and add or hide sections as needed.

  3. Review the preview

    Check the resume content and choose A4 or Letter page size and optional compact spacing.

  4. Print or save as PDF

    Open the browser print dialog and select a printer or its Save as PDF option.

Privacy and processing

Resume data is stored in this browser and is not uploaded by the builder. Printing or saving a PDF uses the browser print dialog.

Processing model: browser-only.

Limitations and considerations

  • Resume data is saved in the current browser; clearing browser storage or using another device does not transfer it.
  • PDF output is produced by the browser print dialog, so final pagination and printer settings depend on the browser.
  • Optional JSON import accepts files up to 2 MB and only supports fields recognized by the builder.

Why use this tool

Private by design

Your resume stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Text that parsers can read

The PDF is real, selectable text, not an image, so applicant tracking systems and recruiters can copy and search it. Its simple, single-column layout is ATS-friendly, but compatibility depends on each system.

Two formats plus Europass CV

Job and Higher study start from different section orders. Europass CV works on top of either one and adds nationality, date of birth, address, an optional photo, and language levels on the CEFR scale (A1 to C2).

Frequently asked questions

  • Is the resume builder free?

    Yes, the resume builder is free with no account, no watermark, and no limit on saving as PDF. Optional JSON import accepts files up to 2 MB.

  • Are my details uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your resume is edited and exported in your browser. It is not sent to a server. Your work is kept in your browser’s storage on this device, so clearing site data or switching browsers will remove it. Use Export JSON to keep a backup.

  • Is the resume ATS-friendly?

    The PDF uses selectable text and a simple single-column layout that is ATS-friendly. Applicant tracking systems vary, so no format can guarantee compatibility; check that you can select and copy the text in your exported PDF.

  • What is the difference between Job and Higher study?

    Job puts experience first and focuses on results at work. Higher study puts education, research, publications and awards earlier, which suits graduate school, scholarships and academic positions.

  • What does the Europass CV option change?

    It adds nationality, date of birth and address fields, places the Summary first, and turns on Languages and Digital skills. It also lets you add Driving licence, Memberships, Conferences and Grants, and an optional profile photo. It follows the Europass structure but is not an official template.

  • Should I include a photo or date of birth?

    It depends on the country. Photos and dates of birth are common on CVs in parts of Europe, but in the US, UK and Canada employers generally prefer you leave them out. The photo only appears in Europass CV mode and you can remove it at any time.

  • How many pages should my resume be?

    One page is the usual target for early-career job resumes. Europass-style CVs and academic CVs often run to two pages. The preview shows your page count, and Compact spacing can help you fit.

  • Can I save my resume and edit it later?

    Yes. Use Export JSON to save it, then Import JSON to continue. Your work is also kept in this browser between visits, but the JSON file is the safe backup.

  • Can I use my own photo, and where does it go?

    You can upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP. It stays in your browser, is resized before it is stored, and is only drawn in the PDF when Europass CV is on.
