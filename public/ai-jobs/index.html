Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding skills for AI training jobs?

No. Most non-technical AI training tasks are language, video, photo, audio, data-labeling, or review based. Some specialist assignments do require subject expertise, but coding is not required for the platforms in this directory's non-technical categories.

How much do AI data collection jobs pay?

Pay depends on the platform, task, country, and qualification requirements. Many photo, handwriting, and annotation tasks pay per task and do not publish a standard hourly rate; some AI evaluation roles advertise roughly $15–$50 per hour, while research studies on Prolific must meet its stated minimum of £6/$8 per hour. Check each current project listing and account for unpaid screening or waiting time.

Are these jobs legit?

These are independently operated platforms, and inclusion here is not a guarantee of a job, payment, or a particular experience. Research the platform and current project terms, look for recent contributor feedback, confirm how and when payment works, and never pay to apply. PDFKira is not affiliated with these platforms and does not guarantee earnings.
